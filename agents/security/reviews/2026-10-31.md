The audit correctly noted no critical files changed, but did not verify the hot spots remain in a secure state. Hot spot verification should occur on every audit, not just when files change. This ensures:

- Configuration drift detection
- Scanner baseline validation

**Evidence**: Report states "Status: Not required (no critical files changed)" for hot spot verification.

### 2. Zero Progress on Open Questions

**Evidence**: Report states "Status: Not required (no high-priority questions)". However, Q2 and Q6 were already answered, and Q12-Q14 are documented as needing investigation.

### 3. No Proactive Investigation
Metadata-only audits should use the freed-up time to:

**Evidence**: Investigation section states "Not required" with no alternative activities.
